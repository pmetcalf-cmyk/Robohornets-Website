Outreach & Mentorship

Growing the Next Generation of the Hive

Every RoboHornets member gives back — because our own program started the same way.

🐝

FLL Mentorship

We coach local FIRST LEGO League teams, teaching CAD, code, and Core Values to younger students — and building our own future roster along the way.

🔧

STEM Nights & Demos

We bring robots into elementary and middle schools each season, running hands-on demos so younger students can see themselves in STEM.

🤝

Rookie Team Support

We share CAD files, code, and build advice with newer FIRST teams in our district — competing hard on the field, helping freely off it.

Sustainability & Growth

Our Business & Sustainability Plan

A program built to outlast any single roster of students.

$

Diversified Funding

We fund the season through a mix of school district support, local business sponsorships, grants, and team fundraising — no single source carries the whole budget.

📈

Year-Over-Year Growth

Each season we document lessons learned, carry over CAD and code libraries, and formally hand off leadership roles so institutional knowledge never resets to zero.

🎓

Alumni Network

Graduating members stay connected as mentors and guest speakers, many pursuing engineering and CS degrees — and returning to give back to the hive.

Culture

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

A team is only as strong as every voice inside it.

Open Doors

No tryouts, no prerequisite experience — any Harrison student curious about robotics, business, art, or outreach has a role on RoboHornets.

Every Skill Has a Home

Beyond build and code, students lead our design, business, social media, and outreach sub-teams — recognizing that competitive robotics needs more than engineers.

Accessible Participation

Fundraising covers member costs so finances are never a barrier to joining, traveling to competitions, or taking on a leadership role.

Representation Matters

We actively recruit across Harrison's full student body and partner with feeder schools to reach students who might not otherwise consider STEM.
