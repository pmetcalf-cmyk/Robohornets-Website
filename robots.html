Engineering Documentation

Our Engineering Design Process

Every robot follows the same disciplined process, logged in our engineering notebook from kickoff to competition.

1

Identify

Break down the season's game manual into scorable tasks and constraints.

2

Design

Brainstorm and CAD multiple mechanism concepts before choosing a direction.

3

Build

Machine, wire, and assemble a working prototype in the shop.

4

Test

Run drive practice and log failures, cycle times, and reliability data.

5

Iterate

Refine the design using test data before locking in for competition.
